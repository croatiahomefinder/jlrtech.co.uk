Symptoms & fault codes

How the Ingenium chain announces itself.

The pattern is consistent enough that most owners recognise it from the first line.

What you'll notice

  • A rattle on cold start that fades as oil pressure builds
  • "Restricted Performance" on the dash
  • Loss of power, rough running, hesitation
  • Timing correlation faults stored
  • A fault that clears, then returns — usually worse
P0016 P0017 P0341

Already replaced the cam sensor?

And the fault came back. That's the classic Ingenium story. P0341 and correlation codes are usually the chain stretching, not the sensor failing — the sensor is only reporting what the stretched chain is doing underneath it.

Before you buy another part, a stretch test on factory diagnostics settles it in minutes. Send us your codes and we'll tell you straight.

FAQ

Before you pick up the phone.

What does the repair cost?

Jaguar XE, XF, F-Pace and E-Pace: £1,799. Range Rover Evoque, Discovery Sport, Discovery and Velar: £1,999. Full kit replacement, fixed before booking.

How long does it take?

Typically 3 to 5 working days. That covers proper strip-down, cleaning, sealing and cure time, reassembly, diagnostic verification and a road test — the stages that get squeezed when a job is done to a flat-rate clock.

What exactly is the Evidence Pack guarantee?

Three deliverables on every job: pre- and post-repair factory diagnostic phase readings, the complete photo record of your strip-down and rebuild, and every critical fastener torque-verified and witness-marked in yellow. If your job leaves without the complete pack, the complete repair is free — parts and labour, the full £1,799 or £1,999.

I've already replaced the camshaft position sensor and the fault returned. Is this me?

Almost certainly. P0341 and correlation codes on these engines are usually the chain stretching, not the sensor — the sensor just reports what the chain is doing. A stretch test on factory diagnostics confirms it before you spend anything else.

Can a stretched chain actually ruin the engine?

Yes. If a stretched chain jumps teeth, valve timing is lost and pistons can meet valves — on these engines that usually means a written-off engine, not a repair. It's the whole reason to act on the rattle early: the chain job is the cheap outcome.

Is there a warranty?

1 year or 10,000 miles on the workmanship, whichever comes first. It applies to the original purchaser of the service and isn't transferable if the car is sold.

I'm hours away. Is the trip realistic?

Customers regularly come from Dover, Newcastle, Manchester, Somerset and London. Long-distance bookings are scheduled tightly: confirmed slot, honest timeline, and the car ready when promised.

Will I see what's been done?

Yes — that's the point of the pack. Photos arrive as the job progresses, and the witness marks stay on the engine as permanent proof of the torque checks.
