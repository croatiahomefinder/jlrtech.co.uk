Is this the right service for my Discovery Sport?

This page is for the 2.0 Ingenium diesel, not every engine fitted to a Discovery Sport. If you’re unsure which engine you have, tell us the year and what the car is doing so we can advise on the next step.

A cold-start rattle or a code such as P0016 or P0017 is a reason to investigate, not proof the chain needs replacing. If the engine won’t start, tell us that up front. A car that already has internal damage may need a different repair.

What the standard job includes

  • Full timing-chain kit replacement once the condition and scope are agreed.
  • Diagnostic readings before and after the repair, with progress photos during the work.
  • Critical fasteners torqued to specification and marked so the checks are visible.
